# Project Statement

## Title
Shop Inventory & Billing System

## Problem Statement
Small shops need a simple way to manage products, stock, prices, bills, customers, credit, warranty records and sales. Manual records make searching, updating and reviewing business information difficult.

## Objective
Build a modular command-line Python application that provides inventory management, billing, customer/khata management, warranty tracking and basic sales reporting.

## Target Users
Small shopkeepers and retail assistants.

## Functional Modules
1. Inventory CRUD and stock management
2. Billing and automatic stock deduction
3. Bill/sales records
4. Customer and khata management
5. Warranty tracking
6. Reports/dashboard
7. JSON storage and validation

## Non-functional Requirements
Usability, reliability, maintainability, portability, local data persistence and resource efficiency.
